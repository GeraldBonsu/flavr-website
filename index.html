04 · Why flavr.

We built it because every other recipe app forgot the cook.

We didn't build flavr. to compete with recipe blogs. We built it because every Monday night, millions of people open the fridge, stare at half an onion, some wilting spinach, and last week's chicken — and still end up ordering takeaway. Not because they can't cook. Because no tool was actually helping them.

flavr. is the kitchen brain you never had. It sees your ingredients, knows your goals, and builds something real — tuned to your culture, your macros, and the time you actually have. No subscriptions to recipe services you never use. No shopping lists for things your local shop doesn't stock. Just your kitchen, your goals, your food.

06 · Help

Questions, answered.

Couldn't find what you needed? Drop us a line at info@flavr.live

flavr. is an AI-powered recipe app that generates personalised recipes from the ingredients you already have at home. You type, snap, or speak your ingredients — flavr. builds a recipe tuned to your health goals, dietary preferences, and the time you have available.
The app generates new recipes on demand from your ingredients. The PDF guides are curated, kitchen-tested cookbooks — fixed collections of our best recipes around a specific theme (high protein, Mediterranean, plant-based). They're designed to be printed or kept on your phone for offline use.
No — the PDF guides are completely standalone. You download them once and keep them forever. No account required, no internet connection needed when cooking.
Yes. The app supports a wide range of dietary preferences including halal, vegan, vegetarian, gluten-free, and more. You set your preferences once in your profile and every generated recipe will respect them automatically.
Nutrition is estimated by our AI using standard food composition databases as a reference. Values are accurate guides rather than clinical measurements — use them to track trends, not to the gram.
Because PDF guides are digital downloads, we can't offer refunds after download. If you have a problem with your order — wrong file, corrupted download — email us at info@flavr.live and we'll sort it immediately.
The app is currently a mobile web app that works brilliantly from your phone's browser — add it to your home screen for a native-app feel. Native iOS and Android apps are on the roadmap. Subscribe to the newsletter to get early access.
We're building a small affiliate programme for food creators, nutritionists, and home cooks with an audience. Email us at info@flavr.live with a brief intro and we'll be in touch.
